Private hot tub

A soak under the stars, in any season

The garden hot tub is yours alone — no schedule, no neighbours. Warm water and bubbles in summer, steam rising off the surface in winter. And the glazed geodesic dome keeps the sky overhead even when it rains.

Roof skylight

Watch the stars without leaving bed

The glazed window in the yurt roof turns into a private observatory at night. Far from city lights, the sky here is thick with stars — just switch off the lamp and look up.

All year round

Warm in winter, cool in summer

The yurts are insulated and heated, and air conditioning handles the hot spells. Snow outside or a July heatwave — inside it is always right. Natural materials, a timber frame and soft light do the rest.

Location

40 minutes from Kraków. Close from all over Poland.

The yurts stand in Przybysławice, between Kraków and Miechów — on the edge of the Kraków-Częstochowa Upland. The S7 expressway ends a few to a dozen kilometres from the yurts, and from Wrocław and Zakopane the A4 motorway leads most of the way too. It is 40 minutes from Kraków and under an hour and a half from Katowice — you leave after work and by evening you are in the hot tub.

Nearby: Ojców National Park, the castles of the Trail of the Eagles' Nests, the Błędów Desert and cycling routes. Or nothing at all — because here you really can do nothing.
