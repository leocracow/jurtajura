Character

The forest takes you under its roof

The Forest Yurt is the one you choose for seclusion. The pine grove gives shade on hot days, shelters you from the wind and quietens everything around. Instead of an open horizon — greenery right outside the window and the rustle of trees overhead.

This is a place built for hiding away together: a book on the bed, steam over the hot tub between the trees, an evening in no hurry at all. In any season — the forest cools you in summer and looks like a fairy tale in winter.

The experience

What a stay looks like

01 · Arrival

The road turns in among the trees. The greenery closes behind you — and it goes quiet.

02 · Evening

Pines catch the last of the light. Steam rises off the hot tub as the forest settles.

03 · Sleep

The rustle of trees instead of city noise. The round interior calms you and sleep comes on its own.

04 · Morning

Light filters through the branches. Coffee tastes different when the forest wakes you.
