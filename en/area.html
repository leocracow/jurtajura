What to see around Jurta Jura

On site and within a 40-minute drive: Poland's only desert, the castles of the Eagles' Nests, the caves of Ojców and a lavender field. It all begins with the quiet of the fields in Przybysławice.

Where we are

Between Kraków and Miechów

The yurts stand in Przybysławice, in the Gołcza commune — on the edge of the Kraków-Częstochowa Upland, about 40 minutes' drive from Kraków. It makes a good base: most of the places on this page are within half an hour by car.

One local curiosity to start with: the only village in Poland — and one of just three in the world — named Wielkanoc (Easter) lies in our commune.

Worth seeing

The Jura within a single afternoon

Distances measured from the yurt. Leave after breakfast, come back for the evening hot tub.

29 km · ~31 min drive

Ojców National Park

One of Poland's most beautiful national parks: the Hercules Club rock, Łokietek's Cave, the Dark Cave, Pieskowa Skała castle and the Kraków Gate.

34.3 km · ~38 min drive

Ogrodzieniec Castle

The most famous stronghold on the Trail of the Eagles' Nests, dramatically grown into the limestone rocks.

Poland's only one

The Błędów Desert

The largest area of shifting sands in Europe — a piece of the Sahara in the middle of the Jura.

13.3 km · ~16 min drive

Miechów

The Basilica of the Holy Sepulchre — there are only two places like it in the world: Jerusalem and Miechów. Plus a modern town park and a salt cave.

11 km · ~13 min drive

Lavender field in Krępa

A purple field straight out of Provence — perfect for photographs together in summer.

28 km · ~33 min drive

Rabsztyn Castle

The restored ruins of a Gothic stronghold above Olkusz — another of the Eagles' Nests.

31.9 km · ~38 min drive

Underground Olkusz

The „silver town” — a multimedia tourist route beneath the market square of Olkusz.

15 km · ~17 min drive

Ostryszni Ravine

A quiet rocky ravine near Imbramowice — just right for an afternoon walk for two.

Also close by: the church in Szreniawa (2.5 km), the reservoir in Tczyca (11.2 km), the bathing spot in Januszowice (23 km) and the Władysław Wołkowski Museum in Olkusz.
