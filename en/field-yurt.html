Character

Nothing blocks the sky

The Field Yurt is the one you choose for light. The sun reaches inside from early morning, the meadow breathes space all day, and in the evening you watch the sunset straight from the hot tub — without a single tree on the horizon.

At night it gets properly dark. No street lamps, no buildings — just stars above the skylight and the quiet of the fields. It is also a favourite with couples who want to bring beautiful photographs home: open space looks otherworldly in a frame.

About the Field Yurt

Yes — the Field Yurt stands in open space, so nothing blocks the horizon. In the evening the sun sets directly across the meadow.

Air conditioning cools the interior, so it stays comfortable even in full sun. Evenings on the meadow are perfect for dinner outdoors.

The yurts are about 60 metres apart and each has its own grounds and its own hot tub. Privacy is the whole point here.

The yurt is designed as a stay for two, with one comfortable bed and a hot tub sized just right for a couple.
